Title: Remote Work Policy
Owner: HR Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Internal

Section: General Rules

Employees may work remotely after manager approval. Remote work arrangements should support productivity and collaboration. Employees must remain available during agreed working hours.

Section: Communication

Remote employees must regularly attend meetings and respond to messages in a timely manner. Team collaboration tools should be used for daily communication.

Section: Security

Employees must use company devices and connect through the corporate VPN. Confidential information must not be stored on personal devices.

Section: Workplace Requirements

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and professional working environment. Company equipment should be protected from unauthorized access.
