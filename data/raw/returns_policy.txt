Title: Returns Policy
Owner: Customer Service Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Public

Section: Return Eligibility

Products may be returned within thirty days of purchase if they are unused and in original condition.

Section: Refund Process

Refunds are processed after returned products have been inspected. Processing may take up to seven business days.

Section: Exceptions

Customized products and digital goods are generally not eligible for refunds unless required by law.

Section: Return Shipping

Customers may be responsible for return shipping costs unless the product is defective or incorrectly delivered.
