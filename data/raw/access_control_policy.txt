Title: Access Control Policy
Owner: IT Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Confidential

Section: User Access

Access to systems is granted according to business responsibilities and the principle of least privilege.

Section: Confidential Documents

Only authorized employees may access confidential documents. Access permissions must be approved by management.

Section: Account Reviews

User accounts and permissions are reviewed regularly to ensure appropriate access levels.

Section: Access Revocation

When employees leave the company, their access rights must be removed immediately.
