Title: Data Classification Policy
Owner: Compliance Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Internal

Section: Classification Levels

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted depending on sensitivity.

Section: Handling Requirements

Confidential information must be protected from unauthorized disclosure and stored securely.

Section: Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for handling information according to its assigned classification level.

Section: Review Process

Data classifications should be reviewed periodically to ensure that protection measures remain appropriate.
