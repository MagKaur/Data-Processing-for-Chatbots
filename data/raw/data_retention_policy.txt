Title: Data Retention Policy
Owner: Compliance Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Internal

Section: Retention Periods

Business records should be retained according to legal and operational requirements. Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Section: Data Disposal

Data that is no longer required should be securely deleted or archived. Disposal processes must be documented.

Section: Compliance Monitoring

Retention procedures are periodically reviewed to ensure compliance with internal and external requirements.

Section: Record Ownership

Departments are responsible for maintaining records and ensuring proper retention schedules are followed.
