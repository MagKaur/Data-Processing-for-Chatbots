Title: VPN Usage Policy
Owner: IT Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Internal

Section: VPN Access

Employees working remotely must connect through the company VPN before accessing internal resources.

Section: Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for VPN access. Credentials must never be shared.

Section: Monitoring

VPN activity may be monitored to ensure compliance with security requirements.

Section: Remote Security

Users should avoid connecting through public and unsecured wireless networks whenever possible.
