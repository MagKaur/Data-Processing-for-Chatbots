Title: Incident Response Procedure
Owner: IT Security Team
Version: 1.0
Classification: Confidential

Section: Incident Identification

Employees should recognize and report suspicious activities, malware infections, phishing attempts, or unauthorized access.

Section: Immediate Actions

After identifying a security incident, employees should disconnect affected devices from the network and notify the IT Security Team.

Section: Investigation

The security team investigates incidents, documents findings, and implements corrective actions to prevent recurrence.

Section: Recovery

Systems should be restored according to approved recovery procedures and validated before returning to production.
