Title: Customer Support Procedures
Owner: Customer Service Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Internal

Section: Ticket Management

All customer inquiries should be recorded in the ticketing system. Tickets must be categorized and prioritized based on urgency.

Section: Response Times

Support teams should acknowledge customer requests within one business day. Critical issues should be escalated immediately.

Section: Escalation Process

Complex technical issues should be transferred to specialized teams when first-level support cannot resolve them.

Section: Customer Satisfaction

Customer feedback should be collected and reviewed regularly to improve service quality and operational efficiency.
