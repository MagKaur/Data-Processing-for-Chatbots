Title: Employee Onboarding Guide
Owner: HR Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Internal

Section: First Day

New employees receive company equipment, access credentials, and an introduction to company policies.

Section: Training

Employees are required to complete mandatory training related to security, compliance, and workplace conduct.

Section: Team Integration

Managers are responsible for introducing new employees to their teams and assigning onboarding tasks.

Section: Initial Objectives

New employees should review their role expectations and establish short-term goals with their manager.
