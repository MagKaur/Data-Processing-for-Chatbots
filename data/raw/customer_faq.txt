Section: Account Creation

Customers can create an account using their email address and a secure password. Verification emails are sent automatically.

Section: Order Tracking

Order status can be tracked through the customer portal. Tracking information is updated regularly.

Section: Contact Support

Customers may contact support through email, phone, or online chat. Response times depend on request complexity.

Section: Password Reset

Users can reset forgotten passwords using the account recovery process available on the website.
