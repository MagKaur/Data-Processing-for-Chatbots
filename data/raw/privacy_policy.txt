Title: Privacy Policy
Owner: Compliance Department
Version: 1.0
Classification: Confidential

Section: Personal Data Processing

Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently. Data collection should be limited to information required for business purposes.

Section: Data Subject Rights

Individuals have the right to access, correct, and delete their personal information where applicable under regulations.

Section: GDPR Compliance

The company follows GDPR requirements and maintains records of processing activities. Personal data breaches must be reported according to legal obligations.

Section: Data Minimization

Only necessary personal information should be collected and retained. Excessive collection of personal data is prohibited.
